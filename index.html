The idea

Stop asking what to say. Start asking what to interrupt.

Your content can be useful, beautiful and well edited - and still get ignored. INTERRUPT gives you a repeatable visual vocabulary for engineering the opening moment.

01 / REALITY VIOLATION

Break an expectation

Show something that should not be possible: gravity, scale, time, materials or physical rules behaving differently.

02 / SIGNAL SALIENCE

Make one thing impossible to miss

Use contrast, movement, color, framing or visual emphasis to make the right element demand attention.

03 / OPEN LOOP

Create a question

Introduce a visible mystery that makes the viewer want to know what happens next.
